Getting started

The Fixotech app runs in your browser, as a Windows app (EXE), and on phones as an installable app (PWA). Everything you do is saved automatically and stays even if you close or refresh — you only lose work if you press 🗑 Clear.

  1. Log in. Enter the shared User ID and password on the sign-in screen. You stay signed in until you log out.
  2. Pick your company. At the top, choose FIXOTECH Engineering (this manual) or FIXOTECH Inno Structures. The apps shown change with the company.
  3. Choose Office or Factory. Office is for quoting, invoicing and documents; Factory is for the floor, dispatch and stock.
  4. Open an app by clicking its tile. Use ← Home (top-left) to come back.
Your data is safe: drafts auto-save every few seconds. Closing the tab, refreshing, or a power cut will not lose an in-progress quote, invoice or data sheet.

1 · Smart Calculator Quoting engine

The Smart Calculator works out weight, rate and cost for cable trays and other products, and builds a client quotation you can print, save and send.

Make a quotation

  1. At the top, set Step 1 – Material (e.g. GI, 80 GSM). This drives the rates.
  2. Fill Customer Details on the left. Start typing the Company / Business name — matching saved customers appear; click one to auto-fill. New customer? Type it and click Save Client.
  3. Use Select a component to calculate… to add a product (e.g. Perforated Cable Tray), or pick from the product list.
  4. In the product card, enter the Design Parameters — Thickness, Width, Height, Side Lip, Raw Material Rate, MFG Charges, JP, etc. The Calculation Details (weight, rate) update live.
  5. Enter Meters (or Quantity). Decimals are allowed — e.g. 0.5, 82.5 — and the cost recalculates instantly.
  6. Click + Add to Quote. The line appears under Current Quote on the right with its amount.
  7. Repeat for more products. You can change any line's Qty or Rate right in the quote list.

Several quotes at once

Use the order tabs at the top-left (Order 1, Order 2… / + New Order) to keep separate quotes for different customers open side by side. Each is saved on its own.

Print, save & send

  1. Approval Copy (DRAFT) — a watermarked draft for internal / management approval.
  2. Verify & Edit PDF — opens the quotation as an editable PDF. Click any cell to change it, then press 💾 Save changes. Edits flow everywhere (quote, Proforma, Indent).
  3. Print / Save PDF — the final client-ready quotation. Print / Save CSV for the spreadsheet version.
  4. Save Order stores the whole quote (with edits) so you can reopen it later from Saved Orders (your Order History).
  5. → Forward to Proforma Invoice carries the quote into the Proforma app.
Decimals: any quantity or metre value accepts a decimal point (0.5, 1.25, 82.5). The calculation and the saved order both keep the exact decimal.
Clear vs Save: 🗑 Clear empties the current quote only — saved orders are untouched. Always Save Order before clearing if you may need it again.

2 · Proforma Invoice

Turns an approved quotation into a proforma invoice (PI) for advance payment.

  1. From the Smart Calculator press → Forward to Proforma Invoice, or open the Proforma app directly.
  2. Check the client details, items and amounts (they come from the quote). Edit anything that changed.
  3. Add bank / payment and GST details if prompted.
  4. Preview, then Print / Save PDF. Save it so it can be reopened.
Edits you made in the Smart Calculator's Verify & Edit PDF carry across to the Proforma automatically.

3 · Indent Prep

Creates the production/dispatch indent that the factory floor works from.

  1. Open Indent Prep (or forward from a quote/order).
  2. Enter the Indent No, customer and the item list (description, size, quantity).
  3. Mark priority if urgent.
  4. Save the indent — it becomes visible on the Factory Floor and in Dispatch for printing and fulfilment.

4 · Data Sheet Prep New

Builds the official Technical Data Sheet for a product (Perforated / Ladder cable tray, Raceway, Junction Box or custom) — ready to print with logo, watermark, stamp and date.

  1. Open Data Sheet Prep from the Office home.
  2. Step 1 – Client & Project: type the Customer name (existing customers auto-suggest as you type), the Project name and Location. The Date defaults to today.
  3. Step 2 – Product: click the product — Perforated Cable Tray, Ladder Cable Tray, Raceway, Junction Box or Other / Custom. The spec fields change to match. You can also edit the printed Product description.
  4. Step 3 – Specifications: fill each field. Every field gives you dropdown options and free typing — pick a standard value (e.g. Thickness 1.6mm) or type your own (e.g. Width "300, 200mm"). Values can be Nil, 0, or a decimal.
  5. Press 🖨 Verify & Print — Technical Data Sheet. Click any value in the preview to fine-tune it, press 💾 Save changes, then Print / Save PDF.
  6. Use 💾 Save to store the data sheet, 📋 Data sheet records to reopen a previous one, ＋ for a new one alongside, and 🗑 Clear to start over.
No office address is printed on the data sheet by design — just the logo, certification, specs, stamp and date. Customers you added in the Smart Calculator show up here automatically.

5 · ChatIQ

An assistant for quick questions and lookups across your Fixotech data. Open ChatIQ, type your question, and read the answer. Use it for "what's the rate for…", product info and quick help.

6 · Dispatch Records

Shows what has been dispatched and lets you track and print dispatch documents.

  1. Open Dispatch Records. New items flagged for dispatch show a badge.
  2. Open a record to see the customer, indent and items.
  3. Mark it dispatched / print the dispatch sheet as needed.

7 · Trading

For bought-out / traded items: maintain the trading item list and raise/track trading requests. Add items, record requests, and keep the trading log up to date.

8 · Factory side

Switch to Factory Side from the workspace screen. The main tools:

  • Factory Floor — the live indents to produce, with details and status.
  • Dispatch — prepare and approve dispatches against indents.
  • Inventory — factory stock in/out and levels.
Indents created in Office Indent Prep appear here automatically — the office and factory stay in sync.

9 · Admin & settings

Available to the administrator account (the 🛡️ Admin button). Admin can:

  • Testing Mode — restrict the platform to the core flow while testing.
  • Access Control — who can use which app.
  • Monitoring — an activity feed of what's happening.
  • FixTech Help — error reports from users.

10 · FAQ & troubleshooting

Q. I refreshed and my quote is gone.
It shouldn't be — drafts auto-save. Check the order tabs at the top; you may be on a different tab. Only 🗑 Clear removes a draft.

Q. A decimal quantity (0.5) wasn't calculating.
Fixed — any field now accepts decimals and recalculates instantly. If you still see an old version, close and reopen the app (or clear the browser cache) so the latest version loads.

Q. How do I edit the PDF before sending?
Use Verify & Edit PDF, click the cell to change, then 💾 Save changes. Edits flow to the Proforma and Indent too.

Q. A customer I saved isn't showing.
Start typing the name — the list filters as you type. If it's brand new, type it fully and Save Client.

Q. The app looks out of date after an update.
Close it fully and reopen. On the web, do a hard refresh (Ctrl + F5). The app updates itself when online.
